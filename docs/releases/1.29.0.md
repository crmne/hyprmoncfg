# hyprmoncfg 1.29.0

Choose Follow global or Fullscreen (games) for VRR, keep Omarchy's own workspace shortcuts when your plan has no numbered workspaces, and get a changed `GDK_SCALE` into apps you launch after a profile switch.

Download the [Linux x86-64 binaries](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/download/v1.29.0/hyprmoncfg_1.29.0_linux_amd64.tar.gz) or [Linux ARM64 binaries](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/releases/download/v1.29.0/hyprmoncfg_1.29.0_linux_arm64.tar.gz). On Arch, install `hyprmoncfg-bin` from the AUR.

## New

- **Two more VRR modes.** Besides Off, On and Fullscreen, a display can now follow Hyprland's global `misc:vrr` setting or use VRR only for fullscreen games and video. The TUI's VRR row lists all five and wraps or slides when they don't fit. By @orshih6 in [#115](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/pull/115); requested by @anthonyma94 in [#114](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/114).

## Fixed

- **Omarchy's workspace shortcuts stay as they are for a plan without numbered workspaces.** A plan with only named or special workspaces, such as `special:qconsole`, no longer switches Super+1 through Super+0 and Super+Tab to global workspaces. Reported by @KamenDCZ in [#113](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/113).
- **Super+Shift+Alt+arrow moves the workspace to the next monitor again.** It passed a direction where Hyprland expects a monitor, and now does nothing when no monitor lies that way. Reported by @cemsbr in [#122](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/122).
- **Apps you launch after a profile switch get the new `GDK_SCALE`.** The generated file now also updates the systemd and D-Bus session environment, so Omarchy's launcher and `uwsm-app` stop using the value from login. Apps already running keep theirs until restarted. Reported by @warfl0p in [#124](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/124).
- **`GDK_SCALE` is no longer pinned while XWayland scales X11 apps.** With `xwayland:force_zero_scaling` off, the scale was applied twice and apps that honour `GDK_SCALE`, such as JavaFX wallets, opened magnified. By @dadofsambonzuki in [#119](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/pull/119).
- **Turning on a built-in panel no longer fails while Hyprland is still connecting it.** The panel could report its preferred refresh for a moment and then stop answering. Verification now waits up to three more seconds for it instead of rolling back and retrying. Reported by @CAI991108 in [#118](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/118).
- **Workspace chips follow their display after two displays swap connectors.** By @RektyRowdyy in [#128](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/pull/128).

## Upgrading

Update both `hyprmoncfg` and `hyprmoncfgd`, then restart `hyprmoncfgd.service`. The Omarchy panel offers the new VRR modes from its next release, and only with this daemon.

## Known limitations

- Moving a workspace that the plan makes persistent to another monitor still fails: Hyprland moves it back. See [#123](https://github.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/issues/123).
- `GDK_SCALE` reaches apps launched after the switch, not ones already running.

## What's tested

The full Go suite, vet, and race tests for apply and the daemon pass. The workspace shortcuts and the `GDK_SCALE` session update ran in Lua against a stand-in for Hyprland's API. The verification wait was tested against a fake compositor that reports the old refresh and then stalls. The new VRR values were checked against Hyprland 0.56.2's source, which already accepts them. Nothing in this release was tested on real hardware or in a live Hyprland session.
