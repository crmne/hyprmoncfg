# hyprmoncfg 1.19.0

Plugging in a new monitor now just works, the TUI has been redesigned, and the daemon keeps up even when a dock takes its time waking up. This release goes together with [panel 2.4.0](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-hyprmoncfg/releases/tag/v2.4.0) for Omarchy.

![The redesigned layout editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/v1.19.0/docs/assets/images/screenshots/layout-dark.png)

## Plug in a new monitor and keep working

- A display hyprmoncfg hasn't seen before gets placed to the right of your current setup, centered vertically. It starts at the highest resolution the display offers, at that resolution's highest refresh rate, with VRR off and plain 8-bit SDR.
- That layout is temporary. Your saved profiles aren't touched, displays you turned off on purpose stay off, and strict profiles can still ignore unknown displays.
- New workspace plans default to Sequential. With six workspaces in groups of three, 1–3 go on the first display and 4–6 on the second. Imports from a single display that could go either way also use groups of three. Plans you've already saved keep their strategy, workspace count, and persistence.
- With the lid closed, the laptop screen only turns off once an external display has a working mode. Being connected isn't enough anymore.

## A redesigned editor

- The tabs are now **1 Layout, 2 Workspaces, 3 Profiles**, same as the panel. If your fingers know 2 and 3, heads up: they've swapped.
- Display cards show the connector, model and screen size, resolution and refresh rate, scale, position, and workspaces. The arbitrary display numbers and logical sizes are gone.
- All six hardware details are always visible. Displays that are off or mirrored can still be selected, and off displays get an Enable button.
- Profiles have cleaner columns, their own automatic-selection controls, actions you can see, and an editable **Post-apply command** at the end of the details.
- The preview countdown is now **30 seconds**, so slower monitors have time to come up before anything reverts. Apply, Save, and Keys stay within reach in small terminals.

![Profiles in the light theme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/hyprmoncfg/v1.19.0/docs/assets/images/screenshots/profiles-light.png)

## A steadier daemon

- When an automatic apply fails, it retries on its own, waiting a bit longer each time up to a limit. Status now tells apart displays that are merely connected from the ones you can actually use, and verification reports failures on every output.
- Reads from Hyprland time out instead of hanging. Telling identical displays apart only probes DRM when it has to, and then just once, with a time limit.
- Background discovery batches up bursts of changes, and results from an older topology or wake-up can no longer replace newer ones. When Hyprland is busy, clients keep showing the last snapshot and try again.
- Both editors let you pick workspace persistence: **First per display** or **All assigned**. Manual rules keep their own flags.
- New and off by default: `hyprmoncfgd --power-aware-refresh` switches the laptop screen's refresh rate when you plug in or unplug power, keeping resolution and calibration as they are.

## Upgrading

Update both `hyprmoncfg` and `hyprmoncfgd`, then restart the service:

```sh
systemctl --user restart hyprmoncfgd.service
```

Existing profiles aren't switched to Sequential. Change a plan yourself if you'd like it. The IPC protocol is still version 1, and panel 2.4.0 needs backend 1.19.0.

## What's tested, and what's not here yet

The screenshots are real TUI captures with made-up profiles and displays. Automated tests cover discovery, preview ownership, retries, workspace plans, and the UI, and I've plugged and unplugged displays on real hardware. I haven't tested every projector, dock, lid, suspend, and power combination.

Not in this release yet: recovering when every display is off at startup or after a failed wake-up, fallback modes, layout reuse, shared preferences, notification routing, Identify in the standalone TUI, and a profile context menu in the TUI.
